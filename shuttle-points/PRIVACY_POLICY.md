# Privacy Policy for ShuttlePoints

**Last Updated: May 17, 2026**

## Introduction
ShuttlePoints ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Player Names**: Names you enter for players in games
- **Game Data**: Scores, game history, and statistics you create while using the app

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to:
- Provide and maintain the app's functionality
- Store your game history and preferences locally on your device
- Process premium purchases

## Data Storage
- **Local Storage Only**: All game data is stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not transmit your game data to external servers
- **No Account Required**: The app does not require user registration or accounts

## Device Permissions
- **Bluetooth**: Used to connect a Smart Watch or Bluetooth media controller for remote score control. No data is transmitted to external servers via Bluetooth.
- **Screen Wake Lock**: The app keeps your screen on during a game so the scoreboard stays visible. No data is collected through this feature.

## Third-Party Services

### In-App Purchases
- Processed through Google Play Store or Apple App Store
- Subject to their respective privacy policies
- We do not store payment information

### Advertisements (Free Version)
- May display ads from third-party providers
- Ad providers may automatically collect device identifiers, device type, and operating system version for ad personalization
- You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings

### Ad Consent & Revocation
- When the free trial expires, the app will ask for your consent to show personalized advertisements (EU/EEA/UK users)
- If you decline, non-personalized ads will still be shown
- **To revoke your consent**: Open the app → Settings → "Privacy Settings" to change your ad personalization preference at any time
- Alternatively, you can reset ad consent by clearing the app's data in your device settings

## Data Sharing
We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your game data with third parties
- Track your location
- Collect personally identifiable information

## Data Ownership & User-Initiated Sharing

You own all match data recorded in ShuttlePoints, including player names, scores, game history, and statistics. This data is created by you and stored locally on your device.

When you choose to share match information, you are responsible for the private data disclosed. The following features may share personal data (your name, other players' names, and scores) with third parties at your discretion:

- **Social Media Sharing**: Sharing match history or match details to social media platforms exposes player names and scores to those platforms and their audiences.
- **AI Coach**: Interacting with the AI Coach feature may transmit match data (player names and scores) to the AI service provider for analysis.
- **HTML Export**: Exporting match data as HTML creates a file containing player names and scores that you may distribute to others.
- **Log Sharing (About Screen)**: Sharing app logs from the About screen may include match-related data such as player names and scores.

By using these sharing features, you acknowledge that you are voluntarily disclosing private data — both your own and that of other players whose names you have entered — to external recipients or services.

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data stored by the app
- **Delete**: Clear all game history and data from the app
- **Opt-Out**: Disable personalized advertisements

## Children's Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not require age verification.

## Data Security
We implement security measures to protect your data:
- Local storage encryption (device-level)
- No transmission of sensitive data over networks
- Secure in-app purchase processing through platform providers
